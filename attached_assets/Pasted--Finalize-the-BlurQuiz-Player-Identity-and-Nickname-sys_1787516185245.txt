foad → already taken ❌

FoAd → already taken ❌

The uniqueness check must be case-insensitive.

Different names are allowed:

fooad → allowed
ffoad → allowed
foadddddd → allowed

Only exact normalized nickname matching should be considered a duplicate.

Do not add random numbers, tags, suffixes, or automatic nickname modification.

If the nickname is already taken, show:

"Already taken"

The nickname must NOT be locked or saved until the backend confirms that it is available and successfully registers it.
