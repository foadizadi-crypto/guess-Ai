1. Level Progression Balance

Review the current XP and Level calculation system.

- Do NOT create Level 500 or unlimited levels.
- After Level 100, Prestige can be added only in a future update.

Balance target:

Casual player:
5 games/day

10-15 games/day

20 games/day

The target progression:

- Level 100 should feel achievable within approximately 12-24 months.

If the current formula does not match these targets:
